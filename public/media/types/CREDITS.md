# Image credits

The homepage uses real Minecraft screenshots from Wikimedia Commons and Modrinth.
Images illustrate Minecraft and existing community projects; they are not claimed
as outputs generated by ModHorizon. No AI-generated artwork is used.

The new WebP copies are cropped to 5:3, resized to 1000 × 600 and compressed.
The source pages below record the original authors and licences.
